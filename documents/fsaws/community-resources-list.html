Illinois Human Resources

Community Resources List

Community organizations can help with employment, recovery, food, shelter, counseling, health care, and other needs. Contact the organization for current availability, eligibility, and appointment requirements. For help finding local services, call 211 or visit United Way of Champaign County’s 211 resource page.

Emergency Resources

C-U at Home Mid-Barrier Shelter for Men and Women

1207 S. Mattis Avenue, Champaign, IL 61821
Phone: (217) 819-4569
Website: C-U at Home

Provides 24/7 shelter with case management for men and women. By application and acceptance into the program only.

STRIDES Shelter

Strides is a low-barrier shelter and resource center serving adults (men and women) experiencing homelessness. Strides does not require sobriety, abstinence, or a criminal background check for individuals to receive services. Residents will be received from 8 a.m.- 7 p.m., 7 days a week.

Phone: 217-403-6150
Address: 70 E. Washington, Champaign, IL 61820

Website: Strides

Courage Connection

Office: (217) 352-7151

Website: Courage Connection Shelter Services

Provides free, confidential shelter and support for individuals and families fleeing domestic violence. Services include counseling, safety planning, case management, housing assistance, and advocacy. Call the hotline to discuss services and shelter options.

Crisis Nursery
